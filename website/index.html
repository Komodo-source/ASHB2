The Core Concept

Two layers. One engine.

Every simulation runs on two scales simultaneously. What happens in the micro reverberates into the macro. What emerges in the macro constrains the micro. Neither is scripted.

Micro

Individual Agents

Each entity carries a Big Five personality profile, an attachment style, a working memory budget, and a dynamic emotional state. They do not follow decision trees — they calculate. Every action is scored against competing drives: hunger, curiosity, fear, belonging.

personality.openness 0.73
personality.neuroticism 0.41
attachment.style "anxious"
memory.working.capacity 7 units
Macro

Emergent Civilizations

From individual drives, tribes form. From tribes, religions crystallize. From resource pressure, wars ignite. No script defines a "religion system" or a "war declaration." These patterns emerge because the agents' incentives align — or collide.

tribes.active 3
wars.active 1
belief.systems 2
tech.inventions 12

Determinism

Same seed. Same history.
Until you change one variable.

ASHB2 is fully deterministic. Given the same seed and the same initial conditions, the simulation produces the exact same history — every time. Change one gene, one personality score, one resource node, and the entire timeline diverges. You are not watching a simulation. You are exploring a possibility space.
