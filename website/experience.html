The Concept

You are not the player. You are the seed.

Most games ask you to control a character. This one asks you to donate your psyche. You complete a clinical questionnaire. Your answers are compiled into an entity data block. That data block is injected into a running simulation. Your clone lives the life your psychology dictates — without you touching a single key.

The Process

Three steps from submission to legacy.

What is your attachment style?

Step 02 — The Injection
Released into the live ecosystem.

Your profile is compiled into an entity data block and injected into the headless simulation. From this moment, you have zero control. The engine's GOAP planner, episodic memory, and personality scaffolding take over. Your clone is now an autonomous agent among 40+ others — it will forge bonds, invent tools, start fights, and die on its own terms.
