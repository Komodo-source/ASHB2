Human Simulation Dashboard Design Concept (Technonaturalism Inspired)
Layout
Background: Deep black or very dark charcoal for contrast.
Central Visual: A large, organic, flower-like 3D model representing the human body or key simulation part (e.g., circulatory system shaped like petals).
Data Clusters: Around the central visual, rectangular blocks of information styled like circuit boards with fine lines and grids, blending tech and organic textures.
Typography: Monospaced or clean sans-serif font in white/greenish tones for readability and tech feel.
Color Palette: Black, dark green, soft white, muted purple/pink accents for highlights.
Sections
1. Central Visual (Main Simulation)
Large interactive 3D organic form (human organ or system).
Hover or click reveals detailed info visually connected to data blocks.
Smooth, natural curves with subtle texturing (like the flower surface).
2. Data Panels (Around Central Visual)
Vitals & Metrics: Heart rate, respiration, temperature, etc. in a compact grid.
System Status: Nervous, circulatory, muscular system health indicators.
Simulation Controls: Start, pause, reset buttons, sliders for parameters.
Logs & Messages: Real-time simulation updates, alerts in a scrollable panel.
Environmental Factors: External influences like temperature, humidity, etc.
3. Footer / Bottom Bar
Detailed technical info in tiny, neat blocks (similar to the text boxes in the image).
System version, time stamps, simulation notes.
Visual Style
Fine lines and grids as subtle backgrounds or borders inside panels.
Text blocks with generous spacing, minimal icons.
Hover states gently highlight data with soft glows.
Use subtle shadows to separate layers but keep flatness.
Responsiveness
On smaller screens, central visual scales down.
Data panels stack vertically or collapse with tabs.
Maintain readability with scalable fonts and simplified data views.
