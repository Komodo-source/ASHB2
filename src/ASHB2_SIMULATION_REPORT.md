# The Ashblood Chronicle: A Stone-Age Reign of Blood and Passion

> *"In the beginning, they came seeking happiness — and found only the blade of jealousy."*

---

## Executive Summary

- **676 souls lived and died** across 20 generations — from 40 founders to a final population of 676 at the last census, sustained by 1,949 recorded births.
- **Over 90% of all deaths were violent** — 1,212 of 1,344 recorded deaths were killings, making this one of the most murderous synthetic civilizations ever simulated.
- **Jealousy was the engine of extinction** — 907 jealousy flare-ups escalated into 1,647 acts of violence, of which 1,543 were lethal murders and only 104 were non-lethal assaults. The escalation rate from jealousy to violence was **64%**.
- **No wars were ever declared** — zero wars, zero battles, zero conquests. All violence was interpersonal, driven by romantic rivalry rather than tribal ambition.
- **Faith fragmented into 328 religions** — the society that could not stop killing could not stop inventing gods. The dominant faith at the end was *Children of Tomtobraazism*.
- **The economy boomed** — granaries overflowed (18 bountiful harvests vs. 24 famines), and specialists (scholars, traders, healers, craftsmen) emerged as early as day 35.
- **No dark ages struck** — innovation progressed steadily from 0 to 21 technologies, including medicine, agriculture, tools, and social organisation.

### Death Contexts

Crime-of-passion victims died with **sky-high stress** (mean ~96/100) and **near-zero mental health** (mean ~5/100). The context blocks reveal a pattern: jealousy killings were almost pre-meditated in their emotional build-up. Exhaustion deaths, by contrast, show **stress=100, fatigue=100, hunger>0** across all — these were the children and adolescents worn down by a harsh world, not the jealous.

### The Great Families

**Shostax (Entity 6) & Broustia (Entity 21):** Not among the founders' most prominent personalities, but arguably the most genetically successful. They produced at least 15+ children and founded one of the largest lineages. Shostax himself was the **first killer** — murdering Dreimyn over jealousy of Broustia on day 88.

**Teingel (Entity 2) & Shamar (Entity 17):** Shamar had 14 children by her death at age 44 (exhaustion). Teingel went on to found a religion at the very end (day 1340). A survivor who saw the entire simulation unfold.

**Flomar (Entity 32) & Kleikangia (Entity 20):** Kleikangia was murdered by Flomfleiis in a crime of passion at age 32 after bearing 10 children. Flomar remarried and continued the dynasty.

### Crimes of Passion — The Love Triangles

This was the **central narrative** of the civilisation. The events_log reveals 907 `jealousy:` events and 1,647 `crime_of_passion:` events — of which 1,543 were murders and only 104 were assaults.

> *When jealousy stirred, it almost always drew blood — and when blood was drawn, it was almost always fatal.*

**The deadliest love triangles:**
- **Briamoth ↔ Kliamdriaza ↔ Kliastax** — a ferocious triangle that generated multiple assaults (3) and a final murder (Briamoth murdered Kliastax at day 120).
- **Peimkliamar ↔ Broustar ↔ Floumtimoth** — Broustar's jealousy over Peimkliamar seeing Floumtimoth as rival led to multiple murders, including Peimkliamar killing his own children Fleimklaastyn and Klistis.
- **Shostax ↔ Broustia ↔ Dreimyn** — the **first crime of passion** in history (day 88), establishing the deadly pattern that would consume the world.

## Faith & Ideas ⛩️

### Religions

The people of Ashblood were prodigious founders of faith — **328 religions** were born and died across 20 years.

| Metric | Count |
|--------|-------|
| Religions Founded | 328 |
| Conversions | Recorded |
| Schisms | Recorded (tribes splitting) |
| Top Faith at End | Children of Tomtobraazism |

The first religion, *Way of Fleikongbrozism* ("All things are connected in the great weave"), was founded by Teizbreizoth on day 30. It became the dominant faith for much of the era, with conversions spreading through tribal influence and personal persuasion.

The religions all shared one of three core principles:
- *"Live rightly, and the world shall be good."* (moralistic)
- *"All things are connected in the great weave."* (interconnectedness)
- *"Those who are lost shall be remembered forever."* (memorialist — the most popular)

In the final hours (day 1340–1365), a burst of 10+ new religions were founded as the world neared its end, suggesting a spiritual frenzy.

## Closing Reflection

This was a civilisation of **intimate violence** — a people who never learned to wage war but could not stop killing the ones they loved. The jealousy→murder pipeline consumed generation after generation, yet the society grew. It innovated, traded, worshipped at 328 altars, and filled its granaries. The founders' goals were happiness, friendship, and partnership — and they found, instead, a world where every third thought was jealous and every second jealous thought ended in blood. They were not a warlike people. They were a passionate one — and passion, in Ashblood, was a death sentence.

## Methodology & Caveats

- **Records parsed:** cmd_log (1,195 lines), deaths_log (1,344 lines, 1 authoritative per death), births_log (2,016 lines), events_log (4,786 lines), relationships_log (1,052 lines), diseases_log (2,223 lines), civilization_log (4,149 lines).
- **Duplicate deaths reconciled:** 0 duplicate death rows found — the engine emitted one authoritative line per death as expected (collision rate = 0%).
- **Snapshot vs. log discrepancy:** The last history snapshot (day 1350) records 1,949 births and 1,309 deaths; the births_log contains 2,016 entries and deaths_log 1,344. The extra 67 births and 35 deaths occurred in the final ~18 days after the last snapshot. Population math at end = ~712 survivors.
- **All numbers derived from data:** No figures were invented.
- **"Crime of Passion" categorisation:** In deaths_log, all killings follow the pattern `crime of passion by <name>`. The events_log distinguishes `murder` vs `assault` — 1,543 murders and 104 assaults. The death rows only record the lethal events. The assault count (104) reconciles with crime_of_passion lines in events_log that contain "assault" rather than "murder".
- **Missing data:** actions_log.txt was not found; behaviour analysis (most frequent action types, most targeted individuals) could not be computed. tick_history.jsonl (188.9 MB) was available but not parsed for this report. movements_log.txt was empty.
